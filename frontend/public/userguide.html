1. Executive Summary & Overview

What is Locus AI?

Locus AI is an intelligent app that transforms fragmented workspace content into a persistent, source-grounded organizational memory layer for individuals, teams, and AI agents. Modern teams and agents suffer from “context fragmentation”—crucial decisions, rationale, and alignment are scattered across thousands of fleeting Slack threads, endless email chains, and buried Notion documents.

Locus AI continuously listens to your connected workspace tools, automatically extracts decisions and action items, structures them into an immutable Decision Register, and exposes high-speed hybrid search (vector embedding + full-text search) so your team never loses institutional memory again.

Core Value Proposition

  • Zero-Effort Capture: No more manually logging meeting minutes or copying ticket summaries into documentation wikis. Locus AI automatically distills noise into crystal-clear decisions.
  • Unified Institutional Memory: Connect Slack, Gmail, and Notion in under 2 minutes.
  • Decision-Ready Intelligence: Query past decisions instantly using natural language, or embed Locus AI widgets directly into your web applications and developer tools.

2. Key Features & Capabilities

A. Workspace Connectors

  • Slack Integration: Automatically watches selected channels or direct messages for decision consensus, voting, and formal agreements.
  • Gmail Integration: Scans incoming and outgoing threads for project approvals, architectural decisions, and stakeholder sign-offs.
  • Notion Integration: Indexes workspaces, project spec docs, PRDs, and meeting notes to link finalized decisions back to primary documentation.

B. The Decision Register & Search

  • Hybrid Search Engine: Combines state-of-the-art vector embeddings (Voyage/OpenAI models) with PostgreSQL full-text search (tsvector) to retrieve precise decision context instantly.
  • Structured Captures: Categorizes captured items by project, confidence score, stakeholders involved, and date.
  • Decision Log: An immutable audit trail of what was decided, why it was decided, who agreed, and the original source link.

C. Team Pulse & Analytics

  • Track decision velocity across departments.
  • Monitor active consensus threads and pending approvals.

3. Step-by-Step User Walkthrough & Operational Guide

Step 1: Account Registration & Initial Onboarding

  1. Navigate to the App: Open your browser and go to https://app.locusai.com (or your local deployment URL).
  2. Access Sign-Up: Click the Get Started or Sign Up button on the landing page header.
  3. Authentication: Choose Continue with Google or enter your corporate work email to receive a secure magic link / OAuth challenge.
  4. Accept Terms: Review the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy modal, then check the agreement box to proceed.
  5. Plan Selection: Choose between the Individual Plan (for solo developers or founders) or the Team Plan (for collaborative workspaces with shared ingestion pipelines).

Step 2: Connecting Your Workspaces (Ingestion Setup)

After successful authentication, you will be redirected to the Connect Workspaces dashboard page:

  1. Connecting Slack:
    • Click the Connect Slack card.
    • You will be redirected to Slack’s OAuth permission request screen.
    • Select your organization’s workspace from the top-right dropdown in Slack.
    • Choose specific channels you wish Locus AI to monitor (e.g., #engineering-decisions, #product-planning, #architecture) or grant access to all public channels.
    • Click Allow. You will be automatically redirected back to Locus AI with Slack marked as Connected.
  2. Connecting Notion:
    • Click the Connect Notion card.
    • Authenticate with your Notion admin account.
    • Select the specific pages, databases, or workspaces Locus AI is permitted to index.
    • Click Select Pages and Allow Access.
  3. Connecting Gmail:
    • Click the Connect Gmail card.
    • Select your Google Workspace account.
    • Grant read-only permissions for email threads and labels designated for project communication.
    • Confirm connection status on the dashboard.

Step 3: Navigating and Querying the Dashboard

Once connected, Locus AI begins ingesting and processing background activity. To find and review decisions:

  1. Accessing the Dashboard Shell: Log in and view the main Dashboard Home.
  2. Viewing Recent Captures:
    • Scroll down to the DashboardCaptures section to inspect real-time AI-extracted decisions.
    • Each capture displays a confidence score, source badge (Slack/Notion/Gmail), and summary snippet.
  3. Executing Hybrid Search:
    • Click on the DashboardSearch input bar at the top of the screen.
    • Type a natural language query (e.g., “Why did we deprecate Redis in favor of PostgreSQL?” or “What was our Q3 delivery deadline agreed upon in Slack?”).
    • Review search results, which display matching snippets alongside similarity percentage indicators.
  4. Inspecting Decision Details:
    • Click on any decision record to open the MemoryRecordDetail modal.
    • Review the full contextual breakdown: Decision Statement, Rationale, Stakeholders Involved, and a direct hyperlink back to the original source message or email thread.

Step 4: Tracking Team Velocity with Team Pulse

  1. Click Team Pulse in the left-hand navigation bar.
  2. Analyze decision frequency charts over weekly and monthly intervals.
  3. Review pending approvals or unverified consensus items that require human sign-off.

Step 5: Configuring Settings & Managing Integrations

  1. Click Settings in the sidebar navigation.
  2. Under Account Settings, update your user profile, notification preferences, and password/security settings.
  3. Under Workspace Integrations, disconnect or re-authenticate workspace tokens as needed.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does Locus AI protect sensitive data?

A: Locus AI enforces rigorous tenant isolation at the database layer using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and ingestion filters can be customized to exclude private channels or sensitive domains.

Q: Can I manually log a decision if it wasn’t captured automatically?

A: Yes. Navigate to the Decision Log page and click + New Decision to manually record title, context, rationale, and stakeholders.

Q: How does Locus AI integrate with AI agents?

A: Locus AI exposes an MCP (Model Context Protocol)-native context layer. AI agents can query the organizational memory layer programmatically to retrieve verified historical decisions, technical specs, and team alignment context directly within their tool execution loops.

Q: What data sources are supported out of the box?

A: Currently, Locus AI supports native ingestion and continuous syncing for Slack (channels and direct threads), Gmail (project correspondence and approval threads), and Notion (product specs, PRDs, and documentation wikis). Custom webhooks and API ingestion contracts are also available for enterprise deployments.

Q: How accurate is the automated decision extraction?

A: Locus AI utilizes advanced LLM-driven filtering and hybrid search (vector embeddings + full-text search) to parse conversations. Every captured decision includes a confidence score and a direct source link so team members can verify context provenance instantly.

Q: Can I filter out specific channels or emails from being ingested?

A: Yes. During workspace connector setup or via the Settings -> Workspace Integrations page, you can configure inclusion/exclusion rules, private channel filters, and domain blacklists to ensure sensitive or confidential discussions remain unindexed.

Q: What is the difference between the Individual and Team plans?

A: The Individual Plan is tailored for solo developers, researchers, or founders managing personal knowledge stores. The Team Plan enables shared workspace connectors, multi-user role-based access control (RBAC), team pulse analytics, and centralized billing.
